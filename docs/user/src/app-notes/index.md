The Reference Guide says what every control does. An **application note** answers a question that only
shows up once you are using them together on a real board — usually a question of the form *"the tool
told me two things and I do not see how both can be true."*

Each note is a complete investigation rather than a summary: the structure, what was measured, what the
numbers turned out to mean, and what to do differently. Where a note quotes a number, that number came
out of a run, and the note says which run.

for another. They do assume the relevant Reference chapter — a note about EM ports will link to
